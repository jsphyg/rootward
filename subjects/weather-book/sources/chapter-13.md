# Chapter 13: Notes on Sources

*Kept out of the chapter file to match Chapters 1 to 12.*

- Great Storm of 1987: Met Office case study (gusts, 15 million trees, 18 deaths in England, later improvements); Michael Fish broadcast wording and later comments.
- Apollo 12: NASA Apollo Flight Journal and NASA's lightning analysis report (strikes at about 36.5 and 52 seconds after liftoff, November 14, 1969; triggered lightning; changes to launch rules).
- Jefferson's thermometer: Thomas Jefferson Encyclopedia, Monticello (bought from John Sparhawk, July 4, 1776, for £3 15s); his July 4 readings from his weather memorandum.
- Schoolhouse Blizzard: NOAA and National Weather Service history pages (January 12, 1888).
- Tropical Storm Allison: National Hurricane Center report (June 2001).
- Franklin's eclipse storm: NOAA Atlantic Oceanographic and Meteorological Laboratory, "270th anniversary of the 'Eclipse' hurricane."
- Ingredients-based forecasting: Doswell, Brooks, and Maddox, "Flash Flood Forecasting: An Ingredients-Based Methodology," *Weather and Forecasting*, 1996.
- Calhoun, Tennessee fog crash: National Transportation Safety Board and Tennessee news reports (December 11, 1990).
- January 2015 blizzard: Associated Press report, January 27, 2015; National Weather Service snowfall totals.
- Hurricane Sandy and Hurricane Charley: National Hurricane Center tropical cyclone reports.
- Cleveland Abbe: NOAA history pages.
- Finley's tornado forecasts: Allan Murphy, "The Finley Affair," *Weather and Forecasting*, 1996.
