## The road to war

The Depression and the anger left by World War I made aggressive governments popular.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1931 | Japan seizes Manchuria, in northern China |
| 1935 | Italy invades Ethiopia |
| 1936 | Germany sends troops into the Rhineland, breaking the Treaty of Versailles |
| 1937 | Japan invades the rest of China |
| March 1938 | Germany takes over Austria |
| September 1938 | At Munich, Britain and France agree to let Germany take part of Czechoslovakia |
| March 1939 | Germany seizes the rest of Czechoslovakia |
| August 1939 | Germany and the Soviet Union sign a pact not to fight each other and secretly agree to divide Poland |
| 1 September 1939 | Germany invades Poland. Two days later Britain and France declare war |

Giving in to demands to avoid war was called {{appeasement|appeasement}}. It failed, because each success made Hitler bolder.

## Turning points

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1940 | Germany conquers France with fast tank attacks. Britain fights on alone and wins the air Battle of Britain |
| June 1941 | Germany invades the Soviet Union |
| December 1941 | Japan attacks Pearl Harbor; the United States enters the war |
| June 1942 | The US Navy defeats Japan at Midway |
| 1942 to 1943 | The Soviet army destroys a German army at Stalingrad |
| 6 June 1944 | D-Day: Allied armies land in Normandy, France |
| 8 May 1945 | Germany surrenders |
| 6 and 9 August 1945 | The United States drops atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki |
| August 1945 | Japan surrenders |

## The deadliest war

World War II killed 70 to 85 million people, more than any war in history.^[n_ww2_deaths] Most of the dead were civilians. Cities were bombed from the air, from London to Tokyo. The Soviet Union lost about 27 million people and China perhaps 15 to 20 million. In the Holocaust, Nazi Germany murdered six million Jews.

## After the war

The victors set up the United Nations in 1945 to keep the peace. Europe lay in ruins, and its empires were weakened. Two superpowers, the United States and the Soviet Union, now faced each other, beginning the Cold War.

:::practice
q: Why was the invasion of the Soviet Union in 1941 a turning point in the war?
answer: It opened a huge new front against an enemy with vast land, population and industry. The Soviet army, though it lost millions of men, stopped the Germans at Moscow and destroyed a whole German army at Stalingrad. Most German soldiers who died in the war died in the east, which wore Germany down and made the Allied victory possible.
:::
