## A teacher in troubled times

Confucius lived from 551 to 479 BCE, in the late Zhou dynasty, when China was splitting into warring states. He worked as an official and a teacher, and asked: how can order and harmony be restored?

He wrote little himself. His students collected his sayings in a book called the Analects.

## Five relationships

Confucius believed society rests on relationships, and that each has duties on both sides.

| Relationship | The senior owes | The junior owes |
|---|---|---|
| Ruler and subject | Kindness, good government | Loyalty |
| Father and son | Care, guidance | Respect, obedience |
| Husband and wife | Protection | Obedience |
| Older and younger brother | Gentleness | Respect |
| Friend and friend | Trust | Trust |

*Notice: only one of the five is between equals. Confucian order is a world of ranks, but the higher rank has duties too.*

Above all he stressed {{filial-piety|filial piety}}: respect for one's parents and ancestors. He also taught ren, kindness or humaneness, and li, proper behavior and ritual.

## Rule by example

Confucius said a ruler should govern like the wind over grass: if the ruler is good, the people will follow, as grass bends in the wind. Good officials should be chosen for their learning and character, not their birth.

## Rival schools

Confucianism was one of several schools of thought in this troubled time.

- **{{daoism|Daoism}}**, linked to the sage Laozi, taught people to live simply and in harmony with nature and the Dao, "the Way".
- **{{legalism|Legalism}}** taught that people are selfish and must be controlled by strict laws and harsh punishments.

The Qin dynasty used Legalism to unify China. The Han dynasty that followed made Confucianism the official philosophy of the state.

**Why it matters.** Confucian ideas about family, education, duty and good government shaped China for over 2,000 years, and spread to Korea, Japan and Vietnam. China's officials were chosen for centuries by exams on Confucian classics.

:::practice
q: How does the Confucian idea of good rule connect to the Mandate of Heaven?
answer: Both say a ruler's right to rule depends on ruling well. Confucius taught that rulers must govern with virtue and set a good example. The Mandate of Heaven said Heaven withdraws its support from rulers who fail. Together they made good government a duty, not just a choice.
:::
